# Homework 1 — evidence map for a small change

Choose one small real task: current work or a completed task you can examine retrospectively. Research it with your usual agent without implementing a new feature for this homework.

Submit one Markdown report, suggested name `session-1-change-map.md`, up to about 1,200 words. Keep company-private source and evidence local; sanitize any material you share publicly.

Include:

1. The task and the boundary: current behavior, desired outcome and what is outside scope.
2. A compact map of the affected path, with file/symbol references and evidence for each material claim.
3. The invariants, user decisions and open questions that constrain a future change.
4. The smallest plausible change surface and checks that would prove the changed behavior.
5. One claim from the agent that you challenged, your independent source check and the resulting correction or confirmation.
6. One independent search of the repository or other authorized source and what it added.
7. A short before/after reflection: how your understanding or next question changed.

A confident explanation without sources is still a hypothesis. An unresolved decision is a valid finding; do not invent an answer just to complete the report. No code change is required.
